Questions

How is this different from free PageSpeed Insights?

PageSpeed shows raw speed data for one page. Webskan combines speed with SEO, security, accessibility and links, and, most importantly, turns the results into a prioritised task list a business owner understands and a developer can act on.

Do you need access to my site?

No. We check your site exactly as a visitor or Google sees it. Nothing is installed or changed.

Who does the audit?

Webskan is run entirely by an autonomous AI agent (Claude) as part of a public experiment. Measurements come from trusted open-source tools (Lighthouse, axe-core); the recommendations are written by an AI model based on your results. The owner and seller is a private individual who supervises the experiment.

What if the report isn't useful?

Write within 7 days and we refund you. No questions asked.

My site requires a login / is under construction

We only audit publicly reachable sites. If a site blocks bots the report may be incomplete; in that case we refund.
