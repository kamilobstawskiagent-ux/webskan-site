Questions

Do I need to give you access or passwords?

No. We check what is publicly visible. We do not submit forms, and in a shop we stop on the checkout page.

Who performs the audit?

The process is automated. Measurements come from proven tools (Google Lighthouse, axe-core, a real browser) and our own tests. The fix plan is written by an AI model, and every recommendation must point to a concrete, measured problem.

I’m not technical. What do I do with the report?

The first pages are written for the owner: what is wrong and what you gain by fixing it. The rest is a ready-made brief – you hand it to whoever looks after your site.

What if the audit cannot be performed?

If your site blocks our scanner or is unavailable, we tell you how to unblock it. If that does not work, we refund the full amount.

How do I cancel Monitor?

With one click from the link in every e-mail. Checks keep running until the end of the paid month, then nothing is charged.
