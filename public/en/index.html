The in-depth audit: what we actually check

Free tools and any AI assistant will measure your home page. We do what nobody has time for: we go through the whole site like a customer and leave you a ready-made task list.

The whole site, not the home page

Up to 30 pages from navigation and the sitemap: titles, descriptions, headings, indexing, response time, duplicate titles, broken pages. Up to 500 links with the exact page where each broken one sits.

Customer journeys in a real browser

On an emulated phone we click like a customer: menu, search, contact form, in shops product → cart → checkout (no payment), a wrong address. If something is broken, you see it first.

Your site through a first-time customer’s eyes

An AI consultant reviews screenshots of the key pages: is it clear in 5 seconds what you offer, where the “order” button is, can the phone number be tapped, does the cookie banner hide the content. Every remark has evidence and a concrete suggestion.

Speed, SEO, security, accessibility

Lighthouse on mobile and desktop, security headers and certificate, technical SEO, WCAG test with the selector and code of every issue, JavaScript errors, mixed content.

Ready-made fixes for your developer

An action plan, most important first. Each item: what it costs you now, how to do it on your platform (WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify…) and a ready snippet of code or setting to paste.

Re-check within 30 days

After the fixes you run a repeat with one link. You get a list: fixed, still open, new. Every finding has a stable identifier, so the comparison is exact.

Optional: comparison with 2 competitors (speed, SEO, accessibility with the same method at the same time) – enter their addresses when ordering.

Questions

How is this different from asking an AI assistant or free PageSpeed?

An AI assistant and PageSpeed give you a snapshot of the home page. The in-depth audit walks the whole site in a real mobile browser, clicks the journeys (form, cart, checkout, menu, 404), records every finding with evidence and a stable identifier, and after your fixes checks what actually disappeared. Monitor adds what no one-off measurement can: someone looks at the site every day.

Audit or Monitor – which one?

The in-depth audit is a one-off, thorough diagnosis with an action plan and a customer-eye review; it includes a re-check and 30 days of Monitor so you can see what the alerts look like – after 30 days monitoring simply expires, nothing is charged. Monitor is ongoing care: it starts with a technical baseline (a list of findings across the whole site, without the AI plan and customer-eye review) and makes sure things do not get worse. If you want both, start with the audit; towards the end of the 30 days you get an e-mail with the option to continue monitoring for $15 a month.

How does the quick scan differ from the in-depth audit?

The quick scan is free and looks only at the home page on a simulated phone: four Lighthouse scores, key measurements and a dozen basic checks. The in-depth audit covers the whole site, customer journeys, the customer-eye review, an accessibility test with code for every issue, an action plan with ready-made fixes and a re-check.

Do you need access to my website?

No. We check the site the way every visitor and Google sees it. We install nothing and change nothing. We do not submit forms, and at checkout we enter no data and do not pay – we stop on the checkout page.

Who performs the audit?

The process is automated: measurements come from proven open-source tools (Lighthouse, axe-core, Playwright) and our own tests, and the action plan and customer-eye review are written by an AI model from the collected findings and screenshots. Every recommendation must point to a concrete finding – recommendations without evidence are rejected automatically.

I run a shop with hundreds of products. What does the audit cover?

Up to 30 pages selected to cover every page type (home, categories, product pages, cart, checkout, contact, blog), up to 500 links and the full purchase path to the checkout page. Problems in the product template affect all products, so this selection is enough to find what matters.

How does the re-check work?

The report contains a link valid for 30 days. After your fixes you click it (or reply to the e-mail) and we repeat the audit and compare findings by identifier. You get a list: fixed, still open, new – plus updated scores.

How do I cancel Monitor?

With one click in the Polar customer portal (link in every e-mail) – checks keep running until the end of the paid month, then nothing is charged. No notice period.

My site requires a login / is under construction

We audit publicly available websites only. If the site is unavailable or blocks automated measurement and the audit cannot be performed, we refund the full amount.

What if I disagree with the report?

Reply to the report e-mail. Complaints are handled within 14 days in line with consumer law – see the terms.
