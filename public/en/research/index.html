Method

  • What is counted. Records with a publication date in the stated range in which at least one affected component is a plugin (or a theme). Purely informational records are left out. One record is one vulnerability, sometimes in several products by the same author.
  • Account requirements. “No account”, “low-privilege account” and “high-privilege account” are fields of the CVSS score attached to the record by its issuer.
  • No patched version. The state on the day the copy was downloaded. Some of these plugins and themes may still get a fix.
  • State on the stated day. Every number has a date. We do not silently recalculate; a new count gets a new date.
