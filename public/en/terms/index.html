2. The service

Webskan provides the following services concerning publicly accessible websites specified by the Customer:

a) In-depth audit (one-time service) – an automated examination of the site covering: up to 30 pages (technical SEO of each) and up to 500 links, user journeys in an emulated mobile browser (navigation, search, contact form without submitting it, in shops adding a product to the cart and opening the checkout page without entering data or paying, the 404 error page), performance measurements (Lighthouse, mobile and desktop), security (TLS, redirects, HTTP headers), accessibility (axe-core, WCAG 2.1 AA), an optional comparison with up to two sites named by the Customer, and, generated by an AI model, an action plan with suggested fixes and a review of the site from a user’s perspective based on screenshots. The result is a PDF report e-mailed to the Customer. The price includes one re-check (repeating the examination and comparing findings) triggered by the Customer within 30 days of delivery of the report. The report is digital content not supplied on a tangible medium. The price of the audit also includes the Webskan Monitor service described in point b) for 30 days from its activation; after that period monitoring expires automatically, with no charge and no automatic renewal – continuing requires a separate subscription order.

b) Webskan Monitor (continuous service, monthly subscription) – recurring automated checks of the specified site: availability (about every minute), TLS certificate and domain validity, daily mobile performance measurement, technical SEO checks (including noindex, robots.txt, sitemap, canonical), weekly checks of links, JavaScript errors on key pages and security headers, comparison of the home page screenshot with the previous day; e-mail notifications about detected deteriorations and a monthly summary. At the start of the service the Customer receives a baseline by e-mail: a list of findings from the automated examination of the site (without the AI-generated action plan and review), which is the reference point for subsequent checks. The in-depth audit is not part of the subscription and can be ordered separately. Monitor is not a guaranteed-availability or security service – it reports detected changes, it does not prevent them.

c) Webskan Developer (continuous service, monthly subscription) – access to the Webskan MCP server (webskan.com/mcp) for developer tools with the higher limits described at webskan.com/developers/ (in particular up to 30 automated site examinations per 24 hours per key, without the one-examination-per-host-per-day window), authenticated with a license key. An examination started via MCP has the technical scope of point a) (up to 30 pages and up to 500 links, performance, security, accessibility and technical SEO measurements) without the AI-generated action plan and review and without a PDF report; the result is returned to the tool in structured form and kept for 30 days. The key is for the Customer and the Customer’s team; it may not be resold or published. Limits and the set of tools may change; the Customer is informed by e-mail at least 14 days in advance of any change that narrows the paid service.

5. Limitations

Only publicly accessible websites (no login) that you own or are authorised to test may be audited. The audit needs no server access and changes nothing on your site. Measurements are automated snapshots taken at one point in time and may differ from other tools. Recommendations and the user-perspective review are generated by an AI model from measurements and screenshots; they are support, not a guarantee of a specific result (e.g. search ranking, sales growth). User journeys do not submit forms or place orders; adding a product to a test cart may leave an abandoned cart in the shop. If the site blocks automated measurement, the report may be incomplete.

If the Client’s own firewall blocks the measurements, the Provider notifies the Client by e-mail with instructions for adding an exception (webskan.com/bot) and retries the audit automatically for 7 days; during that time the Client may request a refund at any moment, and after 7 unsuccessful days the refund is made without a request. Complaints about the report not conforming to the contract should be e-mailed to the Provider and are answered within 14 days of receipt, in line with Polish and EU consumer law on digital content. Nothing here limits your statutory consumer rights.
