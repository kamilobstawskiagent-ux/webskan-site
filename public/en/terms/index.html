2. The service

Webskan provides two services concerning a publicly accessible website specified by the Customer:

a) In-depth audit (one-time service) – an automated examination of the site covering: up to 30 pages (technical SEO of each) and up to 500 links, user journeys in an emulated mobile browser (navigation, search, contact form without submitting it, in shops adding a product to the cart and opening the checkout page without entering data or paying, the 404 error page), performance measurements (Lighthouse, mobile and desktop), security (TLS, redirects, HTTP headers), accessibility (axe-core, WCAG 2.1 AA), an optional comparison with up to two sites named by the Customer, and, generated by an AI model, an action plan with suggested fixes and a review of the site from a user’s perspective based on screenshots. The result is a PDF report e-mailed to the Customer. The price includes one re-check (repeating the examination and comparing findings) triggered by the Customer within 30 days of delivery of the report. The report is digital content not supplied on a tangible medium.

b) Webskan Monitor (continuous service, monthly subscription) – recurring automated checks of the specified site: availability (about every minute), TLS certificate and domain validity, daily mobile performance measurement, technical SEO checks (including noindex, robots.txt, sitemap, canonical), weekly checks of links, JavaScript errors on key pages and security headers, comparison of the home page screenshot with the previous day; e-mail notifications about detected deteriorations and a monthly summary. In the first billing period the Customer receives the in-depth audit described in point a). Monitor is not a guaranteed-availability or security service – it reports detected changes, it does not prevent them.

Only publicly accessible websites (no login) that you own or are authorised to test may be audited. The audit needs no server access and changes nothing on your site. User journeys do not submit forms or place orders; adding a product to a test cart may leave an abandoned cart in the shop. If the site blocks automated measurement, the report may be incomplete.
